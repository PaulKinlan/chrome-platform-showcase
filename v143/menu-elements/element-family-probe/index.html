Element family probe

Four interfaces make up the family: HTMLMenuBarElement, HTMLMenuListElement, HTMLMenuItemElement, and HTMLSubMenuElement — but they didn't all land at once. The dev-trial surface has grown in iterations, so this probe doesn't just answer "supported?"; it diagnoses which iteration this engine carries by checking every member individually, from the early ID-reference era to the current nested-<submenu>-and-checkable surface.

Enable it: launch with --enable-blink-features=MenuElements or flip chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features (the feature is experimental in runtime_enabled_features.json5). Then re-run the probe.
