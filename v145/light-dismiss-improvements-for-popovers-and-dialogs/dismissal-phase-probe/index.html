Dismissal phase probe

There's no API to ask which light-dismiss model an engine runs — but the timeline is measurable. This page timestamps every trusted pointerdown, pointerup, and click, and when the popover's toggle (or the dialog's close) fires, shows exactly where in the gesture it landed. First myth to bust with real evidence: even the legacy pair model does not close at pointerdown — press outside and hold, and the popover waits for your release in both models. The pair and click models diverge only on gestures that never complete a click: see the verdict rules below and the two lab pages.

Flip the model: the click trigger is behind LightDismissFromClick on Chrome 145+ — launch with --enable-blink-features=LightDismissFromClick (or enable chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features) and re-run the same gestures. Older builds silently ignore unknown blink features, so on pre-145 Chrome the flag changes nothing — this page's measurements confirmed exactly that on a 141 build.

Then press anywhere outside — this stage is your outside area. Try a plain click, a press-and-hold-then-release, and a right click, and compare the timelines.
