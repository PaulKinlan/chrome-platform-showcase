Scroll-gesture survivor

The motivating bug: touch scrolls dismissing open popovers. Whether a given engine has it depends on how its pair model treats a gesture the browser converts into a scroll — engines that fire pointercancel when the scroll takes over never complete the pair, so some legacy builds already keep the popover alive (our headless 141 measurement below), while others dismissed on the initial contact. The click model removes the ambiguity: a scroll never completes a click, so the popover always survives. Open the popover, then drag the feed with touch or pen — the instrument classifies each gesture (tap / drag / cancelled-into-scroll) and reports what actually happened to the popover on your engine.

Discriminating gesture required: a mouse wheel scroll doesn't involve pointer events, so it never light-dismisses under either model — only a touch/pen drag separates the two. On a mouse-only device, use a click-drag inside the feed (it scrolls nothing but still makes a non-click gesture) or visit the right-click & drag lab. Flip models on Chrome 145+ with --enable-blink-features=LightDismissFromClick (older builds silently ignore the unknown flag).
