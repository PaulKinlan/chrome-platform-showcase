Right-click & drag lab

Two more gestures the pointer-pair model wrongly treated as dismissals. A right click fires pointerdown/pointerup with button=2 but never a click — the click model ignores it, so your popover stays put while the context menu opens. A press-drag-release text selection moves past the click slop threshold, so the browser suppresses the click — again no dismissal. Both stages measure the real outcome per gesture.

Flip the model on Chrome 145+ with --enable-blink-features=LightDismissFromClick (or chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features) and repeat both gestures — the verdicts change with no page code involved. Older builds silently ignore the unknown flag (measured on a 141 build: verdicts identical with and without it). Touch devices: long-press stands in for right click on stage 1.
