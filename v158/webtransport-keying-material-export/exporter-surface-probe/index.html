The surface, precisely

What Chrome 158 actually adds is one method with a precise shape: three required parameters, BufferSource for both binary inputs (so an ArrayBuffer and a Uint8Array are equally welcome), a fresh promise per invocation, and a pending-until-the-connection-settles behavior for sessions still connecting. Each row below is read or executed against the real objects in this browser.

On builds before Chrome 158 the method simply is not there, and that absence is this page's first row rather than something to paper over. Flag for older builds: --enable-blink-features=WebTransportExportKeyingMaterial, or chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features.
