Suspicious site warnings

Suspicious site warnings are a new protection for users of Safe Browsing Enhanced protection. From Chrome 152, when an enrolled user visits a site with signals indicating it is potentially malicious, Chrome shows a bypassable pop-up that must be acknowledged before continuing. It sits alongside — it does not replace — the red blocking interstitials that continue to appear on confirmed malicious sites. Admins control it with the SafeBrowsingProtectionLevel policy and can exempt domains with SafeBrowsingAllowlistDomains.

why it shipped

Confirmed-malicious interstitials only fire once Safe Browsing is certain, which leaves a gap: sites with strong-but-not-conclusive social-engineering signals. Enhanced protection already sends real-time URL lookups to Google; Chrome 152 uses that channel's WARN verdicts to warn users earlier, with a softer, bypassable pop-up instead of a blocking page — earlier protection for likely threats without the false-positive cost of a full interstitial.
