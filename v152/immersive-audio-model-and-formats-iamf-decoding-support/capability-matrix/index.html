Capability matrix

One format, three different capability questions: MediaSource.isTypeSupported() (can MSE demux it?), mediaCapabilities.decodingInfo() (is it supported, smooth, and power-efficient here?), and canPlayType() (would progressive src= work? — deliberately out of scope for IAMF's first milestone). Every cell is the real answer from your browser for all four substream codecs.

Probes run automatically on load — press the button to re-run.
