Codec-string workbench

IAMF's codecs parameter (§6.3) is iamf.PPP.AAA.<substream>: a three-digit primary profile (000 = simple, 001 = base), a three-digit additional profile, and the substream codec — Opus, mp4a.40.2 (AAC-LC), flac, or ipcm. Compose any combination below; every change runs the real MediaSource.isTypeSupported() in your browser. The invalid-string buttons prove the probe can fail.
