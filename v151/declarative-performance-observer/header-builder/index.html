Header Pair Builder

The entire API surface is two response headers on the main-document navigation. Compose them here with live validation against the exact directive vocabulary Chromium parses — then read back the real headers this very page was served with, including the one that is honestly missing.

Enable it for real: in Chrome 151+ flip chrome://flags/#declarative-performance-observer and relaunch, or launch with --enable-features=DeclarativePerformanceObserver, or --enable-experimental-web-platform-features. For production experiments, register your origin for the DeclarativePerformanceObserver origin trial (see the ChromeStatus entry) and send the token alongside these headers. Activation is first-party, main-frame only: iframes and subresources cannot trigger it.
