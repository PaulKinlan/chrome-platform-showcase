Tap for the hovercard

The spec's own recipe: links with interestfor that point at popover="hint" hovercards, plus one rule, a[interestfor]::interest-button { content: "ⓘ" / "" }. Tap or click the ⓘ that appears after a link. Interest is shown immediately (no interest-delay) and the card opens, while the link itself stays a link. A mouse can still hover the link and a keyboard can still focus it.

    #743 · Hovercards unreachable on touch

    Open · 12 comments · label: accessibility

    CSS Pseudo-Elements Level 4, §4.4 Interest Indicators.

    What happened

    Nothing yet. Tap or click an ⓘ, hover a link, or Tab to one.

    what to notice

    • No navigation. Tapping the ⓘ never follows the link, but tapping the link text does (the log shows a hashchange).
    • Click retargeting. A page click listener sees the click on the <a>, because pseudo-elements are not event targets. This page works out from the click coordinates whether it landed on the ⓘ.
    • Explicit interest. Interest shown from the ⓘ ignores interest-delay, and moving away does not lose it. Light dismiss (a tap outside) or Esc closes the card.
    • Anchoring. The explainer says that after a tap, the popover's implicit anchor becomes the ⓘ itself. The log estimates from geometry which box the card lined up with. Chrome 153 was observed anchoring it to the link.
    • Keyboard. The ⓘ is not a tab stop and does not appear in the accessibility tree. Focusing the link already shows interest, after the delay.
