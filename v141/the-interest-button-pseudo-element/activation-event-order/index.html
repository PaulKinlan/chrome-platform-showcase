Activation event order

Pressing the ⓘ is “treated as showing interest in the originating element”. So what does your script actually see? This bench logs every pointer, click, focus, interest, loseinterest and toggle event, and lets you break things on purpose: cancel the host’s click, cancel the interest event, set an absurd interest-delay, or pull the interestfor attribute out from under the box.

what to look for

  • Order. A click on the ⓘ reaches the host: pointerdown → pointerup → click target the button, then interest fires at the target and the popover toggles open. A touch long-press on the ⓘ fires interest before pointerup with no click (WPT interestfor-pseudo-element.tentative.html).
  • Cancelling the click cancels the interest. Interest is the click’s default action, so preventDefault() on it means no interest at all.
  • Cancelling interest keeps the popover closed but the event still fired.
  • No delay. With interest-delay: 1000s hovering never shows interest, but the ⓘ still does — immediately.
  • Not focusable. Tab from “Before”: focus goes to the host, then straight to “After”. The ⓘ never takes focus and is absent from the accessibility tree.
  • Dynamic. Untick “host has interestfor” and the box disappears, even though the CSS rule still matches (WPT interestfor-pseudo-element-dynamic).
