Does getStats() exist, and what does it say?

Two honest questions, no server required. First: is getStats actually on WebTransport.prototype? Second: construct a real session to a dead local endpoint (nothing listens there) and call getStats() while it is still connecting and after it fails — the promise's resolution, rejection, and the exact dictionary keys are the answers, reported verbatim.

Ships in Chrome 159; the runtime flag is WebTransportStats — launch with --enable-blink-features=WebTransportStats or flip chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features and relaunch. (Per the Chromium flag notes, bandwidth estimates are only meaningful with BBR congestion control, so estimatedSendRate may be null even on supporting builds.)
