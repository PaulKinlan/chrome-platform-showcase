After the end: closed resolves, failed rejects

The spec draws one sharp line through the terminal states. After a clean close(), getStats() still resolves — "with the most recent stats available for the connection" — so a post-mortem dashboard can read the final numbers. On a failed connection it rejects with InvalidStateError. Two buttons, two terminal states, two genuinely different settled outcomes.

Chrome targets WebTransport.getStats() for Chrome 159 behind the WebTransportStats runtime flag (--enable-blink-features=WebTransportStats, or chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features). The failed branch needs no network at all — it targets a dead local port. The clean-close branch needs a reachable HTTP/3 endpoint; on networks that block UDP/QUIC it will land in the failed branch instead, honestly labelled.
