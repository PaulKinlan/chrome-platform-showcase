The friction it replaces

Do the puzzle. The page times you, and then times the token check for comparison. The seconds are the smaller half of the argument — the larger half is that the puzzle is asked of the people who look least ordinary, and the check is asked of nobody.

A challenge, of the usual kind

Press start, then select every tile containing the letter shown.

Not started.

This is a deliberately easy puzzle, and it still takes seconds. A real one is harder on purpose, is often wrong about you, and comes back next time — the cost is not one puzzle, it is the expectation of puzzles.

The check, for comparison

Not run.

The check is a real document.hasPrivateToken call, timed the same way the puzzle was. It answers false here, because this browser holds no tokens — so what is being timed is the cost of asking, not the cost of a successful redemption, and the page will not pretend otherwise.
