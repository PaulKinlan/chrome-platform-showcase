Spatial mind-map navigator

Mind maps, node editors, and diagram boards place cards organically in 2D space. With scroll-snap-type: both, a diagonal pan between nodes often snaps X to one branch and Y to an unrelated branch, framing empty canvas. With scroll-snap-type: pair mandatory, every pan settles on a real node.
