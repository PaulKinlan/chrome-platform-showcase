HDR highlight painter

A photo editor's "add sparkle" tool. The scene is drawn within SDR range, and the brush paints specular highlights from 1× up to 8× SDR white on a float16 canvas that asks for toneMapping: {mode: "extended"}. On an HDR display the highlights glow like real reflections, and with standard tone mapping they clip to plain white. A pixel inspector reads each stamp back, and the export step shows whether the highlights survive toBlob().

Paint highlights

With a pointer, drag on the picture. With a keyboard, focus the picture, move the cursor with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps) and press Space or Enter to place a highlight.

Export and check what survived

Encodes the canvas with canvas.toBlob(cb, "image/png"), decodes the file with createImageBitmap() into a fresh float16 canvas, and reads back the brightest highlight. The explainer says "extended" canvases should be encoded with an HDR transfer function (HLG or PQ) where the format supports it. If the read-back value is no higher than 1.0, the file has clipped the highlights to SDR white.
