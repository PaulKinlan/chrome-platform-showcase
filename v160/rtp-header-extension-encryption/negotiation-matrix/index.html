Negotiation matrix

Whether a call ends up with encrypted RTP headers depends on both ends: cryptex is only used once both endpoints have put a=cryptex in their description. This grid runs a real offer/answer between two in-page peers for every pairing of policies — and lets you model a signalling server that drops the attribute in transit, the deployment hazard "require" exists for.

reading the grid

  • negotiate × negotiate — encrypted. This is the spec default, so two up-to-date browsers get cryptex with no code.
  • disable on either side — clear. A "disable" answerer leaves the line out of its answer; a "disable" offerer never offers it.
  • require facing a peer (or a signalling path) without a=cryptex — setRemoteDescription() rejects with InvalidAccessError instead of silently falling back.
