reading the result

RFC 9335 makes the attribute a capability statement: "I can receive cryptex". When both sides state it, SRTP additionally encrypts the RTP header extensions (audio level, mid, transport-wide sequence numbers…) and the CSRC list. The srtpCipher in the transport stats does not change: cryptex reuses the same SRTP keys and cipher, it just covers more of the packet. That is why the outcome is read from the SDP, not from a different cipher name.
