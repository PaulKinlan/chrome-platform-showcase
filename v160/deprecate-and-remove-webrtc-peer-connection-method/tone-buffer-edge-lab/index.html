what Chromium actually does

Chromium clamps exactly as the spec says (kMinToneDurationMs = 40, kMaxToneDurationMs = 6000, kMinInterToneGapMs = 30) and upper-cases the buffer. Its measured spacing between tone starts is typically duration + 2 × gap rather than the spec's duration + gap, because the engine waits one gap after the tone and Blink schedules the next playout task a further gap later; the timeline above shows whatever your browser really does.
