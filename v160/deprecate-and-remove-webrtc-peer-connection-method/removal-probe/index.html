Removal probe

Two independent checks for the Chrome 160 removal of RTCPeerConnection.prototype.createDTMFSender(): is the member still on the prototype, and what actually happens when you call it on a real, negotiated audio connection? Either can come back “still present” — this page reports whichever this browser does.

No flag previews this removal: Chromium main has no DTMF runtime flag in runtime_enabled_features.json5 and there is no chrome://flags entry (checked 2026-11-22). On Chrome 159 and earlier expect “still present”; that is the honest result, not a failure of the page.
