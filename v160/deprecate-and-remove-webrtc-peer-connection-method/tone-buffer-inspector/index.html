Tone-buffer inspector

Migrating off createDTMFSender() means relying on the spec’s RTCDTMFSender rules directly. Each check below runs one of those rules on a real loopback call and records the browser’s actual behaviour — exceptions, normalisation, timing clamps, the comma pause, buffer replacement, and which senders get a DTMF sender at all. Every check can fail.

Timing checks measure the gap between successive tonechange events with performance.now(); a busy device adds jitter, so the tolerances are generous and the raw numbers are always shown.
