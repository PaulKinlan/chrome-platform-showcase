canInsertDTMF inspector

The legacy method handed you a DTMF sender immediately, which hid an important rule: tones can only be sent once the call is up. The spec's determine if DTMF can be sent algorithm has six conditions. Step through a real negotiation, then break the call in different ways, and compare the browser's canInsertDTMF against what the spec algorithm predicts from the observed state.

“insertDTMF("")” is a harmless probe: the empty string plays nothing, but the method still runs the can-send check first and throws InvalidStateError when DTMF cannot be sent. Rows where the browser disagrees with the spec prediction are marked deviates.

the six conditions

  1. the connection's connectionState is "connected";
  2. the transceiver is not stopping;
  3. the sender has a track;
  4. the transceiver's currentDirection is sendrecv or sendonly;
  5. sendEncodings[0].active is not false;
  6. an audio/telephone-event codec was negotiated for sending.

Chromium's implementation (rtc_dtmf_sender.cc) delegates the check to the WebRTC engine and carries a TODO (crbug.com/891638) for the transceiver-state conditions; the inspector shows you, on your browser, which conditions are actually enforced.
