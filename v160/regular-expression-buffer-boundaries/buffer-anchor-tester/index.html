what to notice

  • ^bar$ matches "\nbar" because m makes ^ a line anchor; \Afoo refuses "\nfoo" because \A ignores m.
  • baz\z refuses "baz\n": \z is the absolute end. Swap it for \Z to allow one trailing line break.
  • Untick u: the pattern now compiles everywhere, but \A means the letter A.
