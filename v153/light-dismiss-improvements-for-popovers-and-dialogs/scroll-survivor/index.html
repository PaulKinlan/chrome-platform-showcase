Scroll survivor

The classic failure: a filter popover over a feed, and the moment the user touch-scrolls the feed the popover vanishes. Open the popover, then scroll the feed by dragging (touch or mouse-drag on the list). The scorecard records — from real toggle events — whether the popover survived each scroll gesture.

Chrome 153 keeps the popover open during touch scrolling (dismissal now requires a completed click, and a touch pan never produces one). The discriminating gesture is a touch pan — a mouse drag that starts and ends inside the feed still generates a click, so it dismisses under both old and new semantics; the scorecard labels the two pointer types separately and never conflates them. Verdicts only come from gestures you actually perform.
