Offline Quantum Grid

OfflineAudioContext takes the same renderSizeHint, with three spec-defined twists worth probing: there is no hardware, so "hardware" must behave like "default"; integer hints outside [1, 6 × sampleRate] must throw NotSupportedError; and the granted quantum becomes an observable timing grid, because suspend() rounds its target up to the next render-quantum boundary. All three run below, without needing a user-gesture or making a sound.

3 · the quantum as a suspend() grid

Each run below schedules suspend(0.010) — frame 480 at 48 kHz — then renders one second of silence-plus-oscillator offline and reads currentTime at the moment the context actually suspended. The spec quantizes that moment up to the next quantum boundary, so a coarser quantum means a coarser grid: 480 → 512 on a 128-frame grid, but 480 → 2048 (42.67 ms!) on a 2048-frame grid.
