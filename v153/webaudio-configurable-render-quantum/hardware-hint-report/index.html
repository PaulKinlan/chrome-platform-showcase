Hardware Hint Report

renderSizeHint: "hardware" is a negotiation, not a command: the browser picks a quantum "appropriate to the current output device" — and on many desktops that is simply 128 again. This page builds default and hardware contexts at both latencyHint extremes, reads back what your device actually granted, and says so plainly, including when the answer is "no difference here".
