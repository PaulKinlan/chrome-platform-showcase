Latency budget planner

A render quantum is a time budget: bigger blocks mean fewer engine wakeups (kinder to batteries), smaller blocks mean tighter response (kinder to instruments). The planner computes the real arithmetic for any quantum × sample-rate pair, and the device panel reads your browser's actual AudioContext numbers for comparison.
