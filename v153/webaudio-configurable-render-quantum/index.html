v153 · Web Audio API

WebAudio: Configurable render quantum

Chrome 153 is the anticipated shipping milestone for the renderSizeHint option on AudioContext and OfflineAudioContext. Rather than being locked to the default 128-frame render quantum, apps can request a specific block size, use "hardware" to let the browser pick the optimal quantum for the output device, or stay at "default" (128 frames). Matching the quantum to the software buffer size removes a source of latency and audio glitches.

Status: anticipated to ship in Chrome 153 — as of this build the ChromeStatus entry still reads Proposed, not "Enabled by default". The origin trial (WebAudioConfigurableRenderQuantum) ran M145–M151 and has ended; in Chromium main the Blink runtime feature WebAudioConfigurableRenderQuantum is now marked status: "stable" (runtime_enabled_features.json5), consistent with the M153 target. There is no dedicated chrome://flags entry: on a build where the flip has not landed, launch with --enable-blink-features=WebAudioConfigurableRenderQuantum or enable chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features. On a browser without it, the option is ignored by the constructor (per WebIDL, unknown dictionary members are skipped) and renderQuantumSize is not exposed — every probe on these pages reports that honestly.

why it shipped

The Web Audio API's render quantum has always been a fixed 128 sample-frames. Hardware audio devices, however, operate at different buffer sizes — 256, 512, or 1024 frames depending on the OS, driver, and hardware. When the quantum (128) and the hardware buffer (e.g., 512) are not aligned, the audio thread must re-chunk and buffer, introducing latency and increasing the chance of an underrun. Giving developers the ability to request a larger or hardware-aligned quantum eliminates this mismatch — audio processing DAWs, voice-chat apps, and games all benefit. The feature ran a Chrome origin trial from M145 through M151; ChromeStatus lists M153 as the anticipated shipping milestone, and as of this build the entry still reads Proposed — so on a stable Chrome without the origin trial the option is currently ignored and renderQuantumSize is not yet exposed. Every probe on these pages reports that state honestly rather than assuming support.

renderSizeHint values

  • "default" — the standard 128 sample-frame quantum; backward-compatible behaviour (default when the option is omitted)
  • "hardware" — asks the browser to pick a quantum that aligns well with the audio hardware buffer size; may be 128, 256, 512, or higher depending on the device
  • integer (e.g., 256, 512) — requests a specific quantum; the browser may round to the nearest supported power-of-two
  • The actual quantum chosen is readable back from AudioContext.renderQuantumSize
