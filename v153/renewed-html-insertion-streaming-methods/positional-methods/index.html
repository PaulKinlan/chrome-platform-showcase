Positional one-shot methods

Ten new setters — appendHTML, prependHTML, beforeHTML, afterHTML, replaceWithHTML and their Unsafe twins — plus the already-shipped setHTML/setHTMLUnsafe pair give every insertion point an explicit, consistently-named method, superseding insertAdjacentHTML's stringly-typed positions.

Flag required in Chrome 153. Blink runtime feature NewHTMLSettingMethods (experimental; depends on SetHTMLCanRunScripts, StreamingSanitizer, TrustedTypesCreateParserOptions). Enable chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features in Chrome/Canary 153+ and relaunch, or launch with --enable-blink-features=NewHTMLSettingMethods,SetHTMLCanRunScripts,TrustedTypesCreateParserOptions. Without it this page reports each missing method honestly.
