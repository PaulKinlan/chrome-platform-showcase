v153 · responsively-sized iframe

requestResize() and the one-shot snapshot

Responsive iframe sizing is deliberately one-shot: the engine captures the child's laid-out size around DOMContentLoaded/load, and later content changes do not move the iframe — that prevents layout loops and surprise shifts. When the child genuinely changes height, it calls the new window.requestResize() (CSS Sizing 4 §5.3.2) to push a fresh natural size to its host. The method is also a strict gatekeeper: it must throw NotAllowedError from a top-level window, and from any child that has not opted in via the meta tag. All of that is exercised live below.

Probing frame-sizing / requestResize support…

Dynamic opted-in child

The child document (meta opt-in, frame-sizing: content-height on this iframe) starts with one 80px block. Grow it with and without requestResize() and watch whether the iframe box follows.

Waiting for the child to load…

Expected with the feature: “grow only” increases the child's reported natural height while the iframe box stays put (one-shot snapshot); “grow + requestResize()” makes the iframe box follow. Without the feature the box never moves and the child honestly reports that requestResize is missing.
