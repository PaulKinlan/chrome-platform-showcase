Gap-attribution workbench

A field trace's worst samples are the ones with no stack: before markers they were unexplainable holes. This workbench profiles ~800ms of mixed load (script bursts alternating with layout storms and paints), then draws every real sample on a timeline — stacked samples below the line, stackless ones above, coloured by marker when the engine provides one. The attribution score is computed from the actual trace.
