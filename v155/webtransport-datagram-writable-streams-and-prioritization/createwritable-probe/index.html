createWritable, probed and called

The feature adds one method and one interface: WebTransportDatagramDuplexStream.createWritable() returning a WebTransportDatagramsWritable. Both are checkable on the real prototypes, and — because the spec explicitly allows datagram work while a transport is still connecting — createWritable() is genuinely callable here, dead endpoint and all.

Chrome targets this for Chrome 155 behind the WebTransportDatagramsWritable runtime flag (which depends on WebTransportSendGroup) — launch with --enable-blink-features=WebTransportSendGroup,WebTransportDatagramsWritable, or flip chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features and relaunch. No HTTP/3 endpoint is reachable from this showcase; everything below is the synchronous, pre-connection surface.
