Enrollment & enterprise policy

The server-side checks only run for users in Enhanced Safe Browsing. Individuals opt in under Settings → Privacy and security → Security. Organisations decide with the SafeBrowsingProtectionLevel policy. Choose a level, mode and platform to get the exact policy artifact, plus a plain-language readout of who receives the new checks.

Real policy, nothing simulated. The values come from Chromium's policy definition (SafeBrowsingProtectionLevel.yaml): int-enum 0 NoProtection, 1 StandardProtection, 2 EnhancedProtection. It can be recommended, refreshes dynamically, applies per profile, and is supported on Chrome desktop 83+, ChromeOS 83+, Android 87+ and iOS 88+. A web page cannot read your Safe Browsing level. Check it yourself at chrome://settings/security and, when managed, at chrome://policy (manual observation).
